# Electrical rules

M5 deterministic rules flag self loops, duplicate equipment IDs/parallel edges, unresolved terminals, disconnected equipment, feeder/source reachability, simple breaker/disconnector/coupler/transformer attachment sanity, ambiguous crossings, unknown switch states on required paths, ambiguous source assignment, and voltage transition without transformer evidence. Busbars remain distinct sections. Rules are warnings/errors for review, never automatic repair or operational simulation.
