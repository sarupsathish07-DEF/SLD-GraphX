# Synthetic-to-real gap statement

SLDForge Symbol-v1 is a controlled, project-generated corpus. Its styles vary stroke weight, color, grids, labels, spacing, and mild render properties, but they are not utility drawings. It does not represent the breadth of vendor libraries, scanning artifacts, field mark-ups, degraded PDFs, dense multi-page schemes, proprietary symbol conventions, or mixed vector/raster evidence found in practice.

The unseen style-D result (0.6333 component semantic match) and the brightness stress result (0.0 on one drawing) demonstrate that the current method is not robust enough for unreviewed use. The product consequently stores uncertainty and provenance, flags low-confidence evidence for review, and never turns symbol candidates into connectivity conclusions. A legally verified real/public microset, an approved data card, and a held-out real-drawing protocol are required before any claim beyond controlled synthetic evidence.
